About

I'm a political science student at Cal Poly San Luis Obispo (Global Politics). I take messy, multi-source public data, audit it, build it into clean datasets and models, and explain the results to people who will never open a notebook. I've scraped and modeled a 1,575-person network, merged four federal and state sources into a county regression, built an operations data hub for a rideshare cooperative, and I'm building a sports prediction-market lab that tests itself nightly.
